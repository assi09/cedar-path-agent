Cedar Path Tutoring - Business Summary

Cedar Path Tutoring is a tutoring and exam-preparation center in Beirut for students in grades 7 to 12. Founded in 2022, our mission is to help every student in Lebanon walk into their official exams and university applications prepared and confident, through small classes, personalized study plans and honest progress tracking. We teach in person at our center on Bliss Street, near Hamra, and online over Zoom at the same prices.

What we offer:
- One-on-one tutoring in Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, French and Arabic: 20 USD per hour for grades 7-9 and 25 USD per hour for grades 10-12.
- Small-group classes (maximum 5 students per group): 12 USD per hour per student.
- Official exam bootcamps for the Brevet (grade 9) and the Lebanese Baccalaureate (grade 12): 8-week intensive programs, 300 USD per program.
- SAT preparation: a 10-week program for 450 USD, including a free diagnostic test before enrolling.
- IB support for Math and Physics (HL and SL): 35 USD per hour.
- A free 30-minute placement assessment for every new student, so we can match them with the right tutor and level.

Our team: Sara Mansour is our founder and head of mathematics; she taught IB and Lebanese-program math for 12 years before starting Cedar Path. Ali Hamdan, co-founder, leads physics and SAT preparation; he is an engineering graduate of the American University of Beirut. Nadine Khalil is head of languages, teaching English and French, and is a certified DELF examiner.

What makes us different: parents receive a written progress report every two weeks; if a student does not click with their tutor, we switch tutors for free; students get access to our bank of past official-exam papers with worked solutions; and our groups never exceed five students.
